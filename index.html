FAQ

What is $FOMOC?

The community token of Fomo Capital. A fixed 6% tax on trades, split 50% holders, 25% developers, 25% FOMO.FAMILY.

How do I earn as a holder?

By holding. Half of all fees collected are redistributed into $FOMOC wallets automatically. Busier markets mean fuller redistribution.

What is the buyback & burn?

The FOMO.FAMILY account commits 50% of its trading profits to buying $FOMC from the open market and sending it to a burn address — permanently out of supply.

Can the tax change?

No. The rate is fixed at 6% and the split is published here. There are no mint functions or dynamic levers.
